# Deep chat bench — 2026-10-22/23

A/B of the small-model tweaks from `reports/Deep chat and research tweaks.md` with
`scripts/bench_deep_chat.py` (gold: `bench/fixture_deep_chat_KI.json`).

- **A (baseline):** commit `3376140` — before the answer-changing tweaks (it already has
  tweak 1 and the safe batch: pre-flight, auto-read, quote check, hit hygiene, tool errors).
- **B (current):** commit `a4353bc` — adds the word floor per source, the evidence ledger with
  synthesis and the stop rule, Gemma sampling (temperature 1.0 / top_p 0.95 / top_k 64) with
  thinking on, HyDE, and the document title in the index.

Both arms used indexes built by their own code (A first, then a rebuild for B).

## Setup (not the GPU box)

- MacBook, Ollama's own daemon (not the pinned one), `gemma4:e4b`, `num_ctx` 32768.
- `data/KI` built for the bench only: StrlSchG and StrlSchV from gesetze-im-internet.de
  (264 + 256 chunks), originals + chunks + indexes, **no wiki pages** (no LLM ingest), so
  `wiki_search` finds nothing in either arm.
- Embeddings: `bge-m3`. **No reranker** (no GGUF, no `llama-cpp-python`), so no rerank and
  no calibrated τ: `_clean_hits` only deduplicates, and the low-confidence nudge never fires.

## Findings

1. **Thinking mode loses on every count.** With thinking, q1 cited 5 sections it never read
   (overlap 0.11), and on 2026-10-22 q2 ran past 4.8x the baseline before it was stopped.
   Without it, q1 cites nothing unread (overlap 0.50).
2. **Without thinking, B fits the budget:** 71 min against 63 (1.13x), with fewer tool calls
   (67 against 75). One outlier: q4 took 1,328 s (16 calls, 2 auto-advances) against 503 s.
3. **Grounding is about even.** Gold sections read on q1–q6: 6 of 7 in both arms (A missed q2,
   B-off missed one of q5's two). B-off also read 2 of q7's 3 corrected gold sections.
   Overlap on the gold sections both arms read: A 0.56 on average, B-off 0.46 (B-off better on
   q3 and q5's § 61, equal on q4, worse on q1, q6 and q5's § 62); plus q2, which only B-off read (0.70).
   Cited-but-unread: 1 in each arm.
4. **Acceptance cannot be compared yet** (see ³).
5. **A's weak spots** remain q2 (no gold, 1 unread) and q3 (overlap 0.17).

## Recommendation

- **Revert thinking** (`reasoning` back to unset in `chat_agent._build_llm`): clearly worse
  grounding and the main time cost. Keep `ollama_client.thinks` only if another caller needs it.
- **Keep the rest of B for now** (ledger answer, stop rule, word floor, HyDE, title headers,
  Gemma sampling): within budget, grounding about even, one clear win (q2). Decide after
  the next run, which must report the answer path.
- Not separated yet: Gemma sampling (temperature 1.0) against 0.3, and each answer tweak alone.
