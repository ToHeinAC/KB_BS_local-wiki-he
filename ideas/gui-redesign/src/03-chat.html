What would data centres in orbit gain over terrestrial ones?

Deep answerRead 3 documents, 11 sections48 seconds

Fewer than the headlines suggest. The main source finds that most claimed advantages are offset by large technical and economic hurdles,1 but three hold up:

  1. Processing where the data is made. Earth-observation data can be reduced in orbit and only results sent down, which sidesteps the downlink bottleneck.2
  2. No cooling water. Heat leaves by radiation instead, at the price of very large radiator surfaces.3
  3. Steadier solar power. A suitable orbit yields more energy per year than any site at temperate latitudes.4

Cost cuts the other way: an hour of GPU time in orbit is estimated at about 3.7 times the terrestrial price,5 so orbit is a niche for data that already lives there, not a replacement.
