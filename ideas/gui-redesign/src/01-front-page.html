Retrieval-augmented generation

Answering from your own documents instead of the model’s memory: find the passages first, then write the answer from them and cite each one.

Linked to 11 pages4 source documentsUpdated 21 Sep

A retrieval step selects a handful of passages that match the question, and only those reach the model.1 The answer can then point to where each claim came from, which is what makes it checkable.

Quality depends less on the model than on retrieval: how documents are cut into chunks, how keyword and meaning-based search are combined, and whether a reranker reorders the result.2
